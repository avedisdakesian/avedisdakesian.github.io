Email signature

Press Copy signature, then paste it into your email app's signature settings. Steps for each app are below.

Gmail (web)

  1. Open Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Click into the box and paste (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

The Gmail phone app uses a plain-text signature. Set it to “Avedis Dakesian · avedis.design” in the app's settings if you like.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Open this page in Safari and press Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, go to Settings › Signatures, pick your account and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, then paste into the right-hand box.
  4. The logo may show as a blue “?” box in settings. That's normal, it shows up in sent emails.

Outlook

  1. Outlook on the web: Settings › Mail › Compose and reply. New Outlook for Mac/Windows: Settings › Accounts › Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, give it a name, and paste into the editor.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then Save.
