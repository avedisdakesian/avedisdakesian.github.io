About

Designing calm, clear products for complex problems.

I’m Avedis, a product designer from Yerevan who has spent the last eight years turning messy, high-stakes flows into interfaces people trust. Today I lead the design team at Ghost Games.

AD Your photo here

I started out drawing interfaces for friends’ side projects and never stopped. Along the way I’ve designed betting platforms used by millions, booking flows for a coworking campus in the woods, and a lot of small details nobody notices — which is usually the point.

My work sits between research and pixels. I like to understand the system first: who uses it, what the business needs, where the friction hides. Then I design the simplest thing that solves it, test it with real people, and keep refining until it feels obvious.

Outside of client work I host UX Talks, a video series for designers starting their careers, and I’m currently learning to ship working products with Cursor AI.

How I Work.

01

Clarity over cleverness

If someone has to stop and think about the interface, the interface has failed. I cut until only what matters is left.

02

Systems, not screens

Every screen is part of a bigger flow. I design the rules behind it so teams can move fast without breaking consistency.

03

Proof over opinion

Prototypes, usability tests and data settle debates. I put work in front of real people early and often.
