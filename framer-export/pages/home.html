Second Caller Experience

Second Caller Experience

When iPhone users get a second incoming call, the screen floods them with options — hold, end, accept — all at once. I set out to simplify that moment of confusion, designing a clearer, more intuitive interaction model that helps users stay calm and in control. This case study explores how small interface shifts can turn stressful experiences into seamless and intuitive ones.

Woods Center

Woods Center

Tucked away in Armenia’s green hills, Woods Center blends work and retreat — a place where people meet for ideas, coffee, and conferences under the trees. I designed the entire digital experience from the ground up — from deep UX workflows and structure to a clean, intuitive UI — and handed it off for development. What started as a design project quickly grew into one of Armenia’s most loved coworking destinations.
