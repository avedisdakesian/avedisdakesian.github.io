Services

Design that moves your product forward.

I partner with founders and product teams to design, fix and scale digital products, from a focused two-week audit to leading design for a whole release.

What I Do.

    End-to-end design for new products and features: flows, wireframes, high-fidelity UI and prototypes, ready for development.

    • User flows
    • UI design
    • Prototyping
    • Handoff

  2. 02

    UX Audit & Research

    A clear-eyed review of your product against heuristics and real user behaviour, with a prioritised list of fixes.

    • Heuristic review
    • Usability tests
    • Interviews
    • Report

  3. 03

    Design Systems

    Tokens, components and documentation that keep design and code in sync, so your team ships faster and more consistently.

    • Components
    • Documentation
    • Governance

    Hands-on help growing a design team: hiring, critique rituals, processes and mentoring for junior and mid-level designers.

    • Team setup
    • Mentoring
    • Critique
    • Hiring
